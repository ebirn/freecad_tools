# Assembly Instructions

## Overview

This document describes how to assemble the example project consisting of a sphere and cube arrangement.

## Required Parts

1. **Sphere** — Main spherical body (Qty: 1)
2. **Cube** — Support base block (Qty: 2)

## Tools Required

- M5 hex wrench
- Flat surface for alignment
- Thread-locking compound (optional)

## Assembly Steps

### Step 1: Prepare the Base

- Place the first **Cube** on a flat surface
- Ensure the bottom face is flush with the work surface
- Clean mating surfaces with isopropyl alcohol

### Step 2: Position the Sphere

- Carefully place the **Sphere** on top of the first Cube
- The sphere should sit centered on the cube's top face
- Verify alignment using a square gauge

### Step 3: Add the Second Cube

- Position the second **Cube** adjacent to the first
- Align edges flush with the first cube
- Secure using M5 fasteners through the pre-drilled holes

## Quality Check

- Verify all parts are firmly seated
- Check dimensional tolerances per drawing specifications
- Confirm no gaps between mating surfaces

## Notes

- All dimensions are in millimeters unless otherwise noted
- Refer to the TechDraw pages for detailed dimensions and tolerances
- General tolerances per `ISO 2768-m`
